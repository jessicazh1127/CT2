some shortcut in blender
i add some furnitures in the car to make it more like a living space, because in this world, people care more about the experience of traveling rather than the efficiency of transportation.
before adding color, the shape of the car, and i want to make it look more futuristic and more like a flying car
I found a car paint material and glass tutorial online, I want the car to convey a sense of personalization and uniqueness, so i make the glass look more like a mirror, which is more like in a SF movie .
the process of making propellers, which is the most important part of the car, because it is a flying car, so the propellers are the key to make it fly.
First use loop cut(crol+r) to add more edge, then use knife tool(k) to cut the shape of the propeller
Then use extrude(e) to make it 3D, and then use subdivision surface modifier to make it smoother.
